# Authoritative Sources & Administrative Structure for Mysuru

This document records the official jurisdictional structure, administrative zones, wards, and civic statutory authorities for the City of Mysuru (Karnataka, India), utilized by the EcoWatch environmental monitoring platform.

---

## 2. Municipal Administrative Organization: 9 Zones & 65 Wards

Under the Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act (1976) and Government of Karnataka Delimitation Notifications, Mysuru City Corporation is partitioned into **9 administrative zones** administering **65 municipal wards**:

- **Zone 1 (North-Western Core):** Wards 1 to 7 (Hebbal, Lakshmikanthanagar, Kumbarakoppal, Lokanayakanagar, Vijayanagar 1st Stage).
- **Zone 2 (Northern Industrial & Residential):** Wards 8 to 14 (Gokulam, Jayalakshmipuram, V.V. Mohalla, Vani Vilas Water Works, Yadavagiri).
- **Zone 3 (Central Commercial & Palace Heritage):** Wards 15 to 21 (Mandi Mohalla, Lashkar Mohalla, Tilak Nagar, Shivarampet, Devaraja Market core).
- **Zone 4 (North-Eastern Commercial & Industrial):** Wards 22 to 28 (Bannimantap, N.R. Mohalla, Subashnagar, Rajendranagar, Kesare).
- **Zone 5 (Eastern Residential & Heritage):** Wards 29 to 35 (Nazarbad, Ittigegud, Siddartha Layout, Alanahalli, Kurubarahalli, Chamundi Hill Foothills).
- **Zone 6 (South-Eastern & Southern):** Wards 36 to 42 (Agrahara, Chamundipuram, Sunnadakeri, Fort Mohalla, Ramanuja Road, Madhavachar Road).
- **Zone 7 (Southern Residential & Educational):** Wards 43 to 49 (Vidyaranyapuram, Ashokapuram, J.P. Nagar, Kanakagiri, Vishweshwara Nagar).
- **Zone 8 (South-Western Residential):** Wards 50 to 57 (Kuvempunagar, Saraswathipuram, Sharadadevinagar, Ramakrishnanagar, Vivekananda Nagar).
- **Zone 9 (Western Residential Expansions):** Wards 58 to 65 (Bogadi, Dattagalli, Vijayanagar 2nd/3rd/4th Stages, Hootagalli, Belavadi).
